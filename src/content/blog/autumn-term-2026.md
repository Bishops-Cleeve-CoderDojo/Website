CoderDojo is back at Bishops Cleeve Library this month, and September also marks a bit of a milestone for us - it's four years since we restarted the club! Thank you to everyone who has coded, mentored, volunteered and cheered us on since that first session back in September 2022.

Our dates for the Autumn term will be:

- 12th, 26th September
- 10th, 24th October
- 7th, 21st November
- 5th December

Spaces can be booked now, via the [Code Club website](https://codeclub.org/en/clubs/b05ca2dc-65c8-4501-bf67-869acc1773c9).

We can't wait to see you back at the library, and to find out what you'll build this year!
